# Jacob Collier Harmonizer: YouTube Feature Catalog

Updated: 2026-07-21

This is a best-effort catalog of publicly indexed YouTube material that either
demonstrates the instrument's controls or adds concrete implementation context.
Searches covered `harmonizer` and `harmoniser`, walkthrough, demo, freeze,
glide, Jacob Collier, and Ben Bloomberg. It cannot guarantee discovery of
private, deleted, unindexed, retitled, or duplicate uploads.

Reaction videos, third-party recreations, and tutorials were excluded from the
evidence set unless they contain an independent primary-source statement. In
particular, reaction upload `tC1rc4Oxpck` reuses the Barcelona walkthrough and
does not add a separate Collier demonstration.
